## 🌟 Vision: Invisible Safety Infrastructure
Neulbit revolutionizes construction site safety with non-invasive technology. We build an
**Autonomous Safety Network** that eliminates industrial blind spots while ensuring total
privacy. Our mission is to create a safety environment where technology works silently in the
background, requiring zero effort from the workers it protects.

- **Zero-Barrier**: A completely non-invasive solution that requires no wearable devices,
charging, or additional training for workers.
- **Privacy-First**: High-precision sensing that intentionally excludes cameras to protect
worker privacy while maintaining 24/7 monitoring.
- **Plug-and-Play**: Instant deployment of high-density safety networks by leveraging existing
site lighting infrastructure.
- **On-Device Intelligence**: Autonomous edge systems that detect hazards and trigger
immediate alerts without depending on central server connectivity.
